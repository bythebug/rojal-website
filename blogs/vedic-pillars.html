3 Pillars of Vedic Numerology

Mulank, Bhagyank, and Namank — the three numbers that define your cosmic identity.

Home›Blog›3 Pillars

Vedic Numerology is built on three foundational pillars — three sacred numbers derived from your date of birth and name, each revealing a different dimension of your cosmic identity. Understanding all three is the key to unlocking your full potential.

Pillar 1: Mulank — The Birth Number

The Mulank (also called Personality Number, Basic Number, Driver, Root Number, Psychic Number, Ruling Number, or Ascendant Number) is derived directly from the day in your date of birth.

The Mulank represents your core personality — who you are at your essence, how you naturally behave, your instinctive strengths and tendencies. It governs the first half of your life.

How to Calculate Mulank

Take only the day (date) of your birth and reduce it to a single digit:

  • Example 1: Born on 23/09/1995 → 2+3 = 5 → Mulank is 5
  • Example 2: Born on 07/12/1994 → 0+7 = 7 → Mulank is 7
  • Example 3: Born on 29/03/1990 → 2+9 = 11 → 1+1 = 2 → Mulank is 2

Mulank is always a single digit from 1 to 9. Mulank cannot be changed.

Pillar 2: Bhagyank — The Destiny Number

The Bhagyank (also called Life Path Number, Karma Number, or Conductor) is calculated by summing all the digits in your complete date of birth until you arrive at a single digit.

The Bhagyank represents your life purpose, your karma, and the higher mission your soul chose for this lifetime. It governs the second half of your life and becomes increasingly influential as you mature.

How to Calculate Bhagyank

Add every single digit in your complete date of birth (DD/MM/YYYY), keep reducing until you reach a single digit:

  • Example 1: 23/09/1996 → 2+3+0+9+1+9+9+6 = 39 → 3+9 = 12 → 1+2 = 3 → Bhagyank is 3
  • Example 2: 07/12/1994 → 0+7+1+2+1+9+9+4 = 33 → 3+3 = 6 → Bhagyank is 6

Bhagyank is always a single digit from 1 to 9. Bhagyank cannot be changed.

Pillar 3: Namank — The Name Number

The Namank is the sum of the numerological values assigned to each letter in your full name. Unlike Mulank and Bhagyank, the Namank can be changed through name correction — making it the most actionable of the three pillars.

In Vedic Numerology, each alphabet is assigned a specific number based on the planetary system:

Vedic Numerology — Number Meanings 1 to 9
  • 1 — Sun (Surya)
  • 2 — Moon (Chandra)
  • 3 — Jupiter (Guru / Brahaspati)
  • 4 — Rahu (North Node)
  • 5 — Mercury (Buddh)
  • 6 — Venus (Shukra)
  • 7 — Ketu (South Node)
  • 8 — Saturn (Shani)
  • 9 — Mars (Mangal)

Note: In Western Numerology, Rahu stands for Uranus and Ketu stands for Neptune.

Which Number is Most Important?

Both Mulank and Bhagyank are critically important, but they govern different phases of life:

  • First half of life: Mulank (birth number) is dominant — it shapes your instincts, personality, and early experiences.
  • Second half of life: Bhagyank (destiny number) becomes more prominent — it governs your life purpose, karma, and mature path.

The Namank bridges both — a well-aligned name number can enhance both your Mulank and Bhagyank energies, while a misaligned name can create friction. This is why name correction is such a powerful tool in Vedic Numerology.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is Mulank in Vedic Numerology? Mulank stands for Basic Number, Personality Number, or Birth Number.
  • How is Bhagyank calculated? Bhagyank is the sum of all digits in your complete date of birth, reduced to a single digit.
  • Which is more important — birth number or destiny number? In the first half of your life, the birth number rules. In the second half, the destiny number takes over.
  • Can I change my destiny with my name number? Yes — name correction through Namank is one of the most powerful interventions in Vedic Numerology.
  • What are the 3 pillars of Vedic Numerology? Birth number (Mulank), Destiny number (Bhagyank), and Name number (Namank).

Mulank 9 · Mars

Personality Number 9

The Fearless Warrior — courageous, determined, and built to conquer.

Home›Blog›Mulank 9
♂ Ruling Planet: Mars (Mangal) Birth Dates: 9th, 18th, 27th of any month

Personality number or Mulank 9 is the root number of those born on the 9th, 18th, and 27th of any month. These people are governed by the planet Mars (Mangal) — the warrior planet, the planet of raw courage, determination, and unstoppable action. Their personality, career paths, and life lessons pulse with Martian fire.

✦ Quick Reference
  • Ruling Planet: Mars (Mangal)
  • Lucky Day: Tuesday
  • Gemstone: Red Coral (Moonga)
  • Lucky Colours: Crimson, Scarlet, Brick Red
  • Mantra: Om Kraam Kreem Kraum Sah Bhaumaaya Namah (108 times)

Strengths of Mulank 9

Mars gives Mulank 9 individuals an extraordinary reservoir of courage, energy, and willpower. They are the warriors of numerology — they don't just fight, they win. Key strengths:

  • Pure (Satvik) Energy: Despite their fierce exterior, they carry a deeply pure intention.
  • Hardworking: Relentless work ethic — they outwork everyone around them.
  • Determined: Once they set a goal, nothing stops them.
  • Dedicated: Fully committed to whatever they take on.
  • Strong Will Power: Can withstand pressure and adversity that would break others.
  • Never-Give-Up Attitude: Failure is just a detour, never a destination.
  • Extremely Helpful: Despite their tough image, they go out of their way for others.
  • No Fear: They walk into uncertainty without flinching.
  • Can Rebuild: Resilient beyond measure — they rise from setbacks stronger.
  • Action Oriented: They don't just plan — they execute, immediately.
  • Confident & Motivated: Self-assured and energised by challenge.
  • Competitive: Thrive in environments where performance is measured.
  • Extreme Energy: Physical and mental vitality that amazes those around them.

Weaknesses of Mulank 9

Mars's fire can burn if uncontrolled. Mulank 9 must watch for:

  • Anger: A fierce, fast-burning temper — can damage relationships.
  • Stubbornness: Absolute conviction in their own way, even when wrong.
  • Aggressiveness: Can come across as combative or intimidating.
  • Impulsiveness: Quick to act without thinking through consequences.
  • Early Judgement: Forms strong first impressions that are hard to reverse.
  • Fierce but Soft: Behind the warrior exterior lies a surprisingly tender heart — vulnerability they rarely show.

Flourishing Career Options

Mars rules action, combat, surgery, engineering, and leadership under fire. Mulank 9 individuals naturally excel in high-intensity, high-stakes environments:

  • Defence, Army, Police, Military
  • Surgery — heart specialist, bone specialist, dentist, surgeon
  • CEO, CFO, and high-level executive leadership
  • Automobile industry and machinery
  • Law enforcement and legal practice
  • Sports, racing, and competitive athletics
  • Business operations and factory management
  • Restaurants, gym, and fitness industry
  • Occult and spiritual healing

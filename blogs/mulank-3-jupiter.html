Mulank 3 · Jupiter

Personality Number 3

The King-Maker — wise, strategic, and deeply spiritual.

Home›Blog›Mulank 3
♃ Ruling Planet: Jupiter (Guru / Brahaspati) Birth Dates: 3rd, 12th, 21st, 30th of any month

Personality number or Mulank 3 is the root number of those born on the 3rd, 12th, 21st, and 30th of any month. These people are governed by the planet Jupiter (Guru or Brahaspati) — the teacher of the Gods, the most auspicious planet in Vedic astrology. Their personality, career paths, and life lessons are guided by Jupiterian wisdom.

✦ Quick Reference
  • Ruling Planet: Jupiter (Guru)
  • Lucky Day: Thursday
  • Gemstone: Yellow Sapphire (Pukhraj)
  • Lucky Colour: Yellow
  • Mantra: Om Gurave Namah (108 times)

Strengths of Mulank 3

Jupiter blesses Mulank 3 individuals with extraordinary wisdom, generosity, and moral character. They are natural king-makers — the power behind great leaders and movements. Key strengths:

  • King-Makers: They uplift and empower others to reach great heights.
  • Master of Influence: Skilled at working with people to achieve grand goals.
  • Long-Term Vision: They plan years, even decades ahead.
  • Good Ethics & High Moral Values: A strong sense of right and wrong.
  • Optimistic: Always see the silver lining, even in dark times.
  • Strategic Thinkers: Able to see the big picture and plan accordingly.
  • Familial: Deep bonds with family and a strong sense of duty.
  • Spiritual: Naturally drawn to higher wisdom and spiritual practices.
  • Helpful Nature: Selfless givers who genuinely want to help others.
  • Truthful & Loyal: Honest to a fault and fiercely loyal.
  • Wisdom & Generosity: Freely share knowledge and resources.
  • Great Counsellors & Communicators: Natural advisors and storytellers.

Weaknesses of Mulank 3

Jupiter's shadow brings excess and complacency. Mulank 3 must watch for:

  • Laziness: The comfort of wisdom can breed inaction.
  • Misfortune: Despite great ability, life can present unexpected setbacks.
  • Stubbornness: Fixed in their philosophy, resistant to other perspectives.
  • Overconfidence: Can underestimate challenges due to natural optimism.

Flourishing Career Options

Jupiter governs higher knowledge, law, spirituality, and guidance. Mulank 3 individuals flourish in roles where they can teach, guide, and inspire:

  • Teachers, professors, and educators
  • Guides, coaches, and mentors
  • Counsellors and therapists
  • Managers and strategic advisors
  • Lawyers and legal professionals
  • Spiritual leaders and practitioners

The Story of Rahu & Ketu

Shadow Planets (Chaya Graha) — the cosmic tale behind numerology numbers 4 and 7.

Home›Blog›Rahu & Ketu

In Vedic Numerology, the numbers 4 and 7 are governed not by physical planets but by Rahu and Ketu — the shadow planets, also called Chaya Graha. To understand why, we must journey back to one of the most magnificent stories in Hindu mythology: the Samudra Manthan — the churning of the cosmic ocean.

The Samudra Manthan — Churning the Ocean of Creation

In the quest for Amrit — the nectar of immortality — the great Samudra Manthan (churning of the cosmic ocean) was undertaken. The great Dronagiri Parvat (Himalayan mountain) was used as the churning rod. The mighty serpent Vasuki Naag was coiled around it as the rope.

Lord Vishnu took his second avatar — the Kurma Avatar (the divine tortoise) — and balanced the mountain on his back. The Devtas (Gods) held the tail of Vasuki, while the Rakshasas (Demons) held the head, and together they began the great churning.

What Emerged from the Ocean

Initially, the ocean yielded wondrous treasures one by one — gold, silver, precious metals, and gemstones. But then came something terrifying: Halahala — a deadly poison so potent that even its vapors threatened to destroy all of creation.

In the panic that followed, Lord Shiva stepped forward and drank the poison. His beloved consort Mata Parvati immediately pressed her hand around his throat to prevent the poison from reaching his body. It lodged in his neck, turning it a deep blue — and from that day, Shiva earned the name Neelkanth (the Blue-Throated One).

The Birth of Amrit — and the Great Deception

Finally, after enormous effort, Amrit — the nectar of immortality — emerged from the depths. Both the Devtas and the Rakshasas wanted it first, and chaos threatened to break out.

Lord Vishnu intervened, this time taking the form of the mesmerising Mohini Avatar — a beautiful enchantress whose appearance bewitched everyone present. Both Gods and Demons agreed to let Mohini decide the order of serving the Amrit.

Mohini began serving Amrit exclusively to the Devtas. Among the Rakshasas, one demon was shrewder than the rest — Swarbhanu. Realising Mohini's strategy, he disguised himself as a Devta and slipped into the divine row, positioning himself between Surya (Sun) and Chandra (Moon).

Unaware of the deception, Mohini served the Amrit to Swarbhanu. Just as it passed his lips, Surya and Chandra recognised the demon and cried out in alarm. Mohini immediately dropped the divine disguise, revealing Lord Vishnu in his full cosmic form — and in one swift motion, his Sudarshan Chakra flew and severed Swarbhanu's head from his body.

The Creation of Rahu and Ketu

But the Amrit had already passed into Swarbhanu's throat. The moment it touched his being, he became immortal — even as his body was severed in two. The chakra that split him could never be undone.

The head became Rahu.
The body became Ketu.

This is why Rahu and Ketu are not physical planets but shadow entities — they are mathematically calculated points that represent the two nodes of the Moon's orbit. They cast influence without physical form, moving always in retrograde, governing karma, illusion, and spiritual transformation.

Rahu & Ketu in Vedic Numerology
  • Rahu governs Number 4 — transformation, illusion, unconventional thinking, ambition, and breaking of norms.
  • Ketu governs Number 7 — spirituality, detachment, inner wisdom, intuition, and moksha (liberation).
  • In Western Numerology, Rahu is equivalent to Uranus and Ketu to Neptune.

Mulank 8 · Saturn

Personality Number 8

The Patient Builder — disciplined, empathetic, and built for endurance.

Home›Blog›Mulank 8
♄ Ruling Planet: Saturn (Shani) Birth Dates: 8th, 17th, 26th of any month

Personality number or Mulank 8 is the root number of those born on the 8th, 17th, and 26th of any month. These people are governed by the planet Saturn (Shani) — the taskmaster of the solar system, the planet of karma, discipline, and justice. Their personality, career paths, and life lessons are all governed by Saturnian energy.

✦ Quick Reference
  • Ruling Planet: Saturn (Shani)
  • Lucky Day: Saturday
  • Gemstone: Blue Sapphire (Neelam)
  • Lucky Colours: Black (only on Saturdays), Navy Blue, Dark Blue
  • Mantra: Om Sham Shanaishcharaya Namah (108 times)

Strengths of Mulank 8

Saturn bestows Mulank 8 individuals with incredible endurance, discipline, and a powerful sense of justice. They are the workers of the numerological world — consistent and dependable. Key strengths:

  • Discipline: Unwavering commitment to structure and routine.
  • Consistency: Show up day after day, regardless of the outcome.
  • Focus: Ability to concentrate deeply on long-term goals.
  • Organised: Natural ability to create order from chaos.
  • Empathetic: Surprisingly compassionate beneath their tough exterior.
  • Patient: Willing to wait as long as it takes for the right result.
  • Hardworking: Saturn demands effort, and Mulank 8 delivers it.
  • Sense of Justice: Strong moral compass — deeply bothered by unfairness.
  • Spiritual: Often drawn to deeper spiritual truths after life challenges.
  • Good Listeners: People trust them with their deepest concerns.
  • Practical: Grounded, realistic thinkers who deal in facts.
  • Loyal: Once committed, they are deeply faithful and reliable.

Weaknesses of Mulank 8

Saturn's weight can become burdensome. Mulank 8 must watch for:

  • Procrastination: Despite discipline, can put off emotionally difficult tasks.
  • Impatience: When progress feels slow, frustration builds.
  • Cold-heartedness: Emotional walls can make them seem distant or harsh.
  • Feeling Overburdened: Takes on too much responsibility — often for others.
  • Frustration: Chronic dissatisfaction when life doesn't meet their high standards.
  • Early Judgement: Can form rigid opinions of people too quickly.

Flourishing Career Options

Saturn rules law, structure, authority, and long-term building. Mulank 8 individuals naturally excel in:

  • Administrative and government sectors
  • Law and justice — judiciary, legal practice
  • Real estate and construction
  • Finance and banking
  • Academics and research

Daily Mantra for Mulank 8

Recommended Mantra

ॐ शं शनैश्चराय नमः

Om Sham Shanaishcharaya Namah

  • Chant this mantra 108 times daily to appease Saturn and harness its positive energy.
  • Best chanted on Saturdays, ideally at sunrise or sunset.

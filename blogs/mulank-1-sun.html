Mulank 1 · Sun

Personality Number 1

The Solar Leader — born to lead, born to shine.

Home›Blog›Mulank 1
Ruling Planet: Sun (Surya / Aditya) Birth Dates: 1st, 10th, 19th, 28th of any month

Personality number or Mulank 1 is the root number of those born on the 1st, 10th, 19th, and 28th of any month. These people are governed by the planet Sun (Surya or Aditya). Their personality, career paths, and life lessons are all ruled by the Solar energy — the most powerful energy in the cosmos.

✦ Quick Reference
  • Ruling Planet: Sun (Surya)
  • Lucky Day: Sunday
  • Gemstone: Ruby
  • Lucky Colour: Gold
  • Mantra: Om Suryaya Namah (108 times)

Strengths of Mulank 1

People with Mulank 1 carry an innate solar power within them. The Sun is the centre of our solar system, and similarly, Mulank 1 individuals tend to become the centre of attraction wherever they go. Their key strengths include:

  • Born Leader: Natural leadership ability — people instinctively look to them for direction.
  • Independent: Self-reliant and capable of building their own path.
  • Center of Attraction: Charismatic and magnetic presence in any room.
  • Self-Motivated: Internal drive — they don't need external validation to take action.
  • Confident: High self-confidence that radiates to others.
  • Innovative: Creative thinkers who approach problems with original solutions.
  • Ambitious: Big dreams and the drive to pursue them relentlessly.
  • High Energy: Dynamic, full of vitality and enthusiasm.
  • Optimistic: Positive outlook, even in challenging times.
  • Authoritative: Naturally commanding — people listen when they speak.

Weaknesses of Mulank 1

Like the Sun itself, Mulank 1 can be blinding in excess. The shadow side includes:

  • Anger: A fiery temper that can flare quickly when challenged.
  • Stubbornness: Difficulty changing course once a decision is made.
  • Dilemma: Struggle with indecision when forced to choose between two strong paths.
  • Dictatorship: Leadership can tip into controlling or domineering behaviour.
  • Selfishness: Tendency to prioritise personal goals over others' needs.
  • Ego: Pride can become a barrier to collaboration and growth.
  • Dominating: Can unintentionally overshadow those around them.

Flourishing Career Options

With solar energy driving ambition and leadership, Mulank 1 individuals thrive in roles where they can lead, innovate, and be visible. The best career paths are:

  • Entrepreneurship — founders, startup leaders
  • High-level executive or C-suite roles
  • Consulting and advisory roles
  • Leadership and administrative positions
  • Politics and public governance

Daily Mantra for Mulank 1

Recommended Mantra

ॐ सूर्याय नमः

Om Suryaya Namah

  • Chant this mantra 108 times daily to strengthen the Sun's energy in your life.
  • Best chanted on Sundays, ideally at sunrise facing the east.
